# Privacy Policy - Personal Shorts Uploader

Last updated: 26 October 2026

**Who this applies to.** "Personal Shorts Uploader" is a private automation tool operated by one individual (Anduamlak Yalewelet Tarekegne, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia) to upload videos to the operator's own YouTube channel. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.

**Data accessed.** The tool uses YouTube API Services (videos.insert) with OAuth authorization granted by the operator's own Google account. It does not read, collect or store data about any other person, viewer or subscriber.

**Data stored.** Only locally on the operator's computer: OAuth tokens (inside the operator's own n8n installation), rendered video files, and a log of which stock clips and uploads have been used. Nothing is sent to third parties except the videos uploaded to YouTube and the requests to the footage and AI services used to create them.

**Sharing and sale.** No data is shared with, sold to or transferred to any third party.

**YouTube / Google.** The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the operator is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy). Access can be revoked at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
